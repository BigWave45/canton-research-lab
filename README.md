# Canton Research Lab

Private research workspace for an interdisciplinary, quantitatively grounded study of Canton Network and institutional financial infrastructure.

## Research orientation

- **Core lens:** interdisciplinary research across financial economics, institutional finance, mathematics, and distributed systems.
- **Analytical foundation:** quantitative methods where the question and available evidence justify them.
- **Primary output:** a research paper supported by reproducible calculations, documented datasets, models, and visualisations.
- **Grant context:** Canton Community Research Grants. The programme's official announcement and current terms must be checked before any eligibility or submission decision.

## Working principles

1. Investigate before selecting a topic.
2. Study funded projects and their completed outputs, not only grant announcements.
3. Separate documented facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns.
4. Use primary sources and preserve provenance.
5. Do not invent data, citations, grant criteria, or probabilities of selection.
6. Use mathematics to answer a real research question, not as decoration.
7. Preserve reproducibility: data definitions, transformations, assumptions, code/calculations, and limitations.
8. Keep unpublished research and working materials private.

## Repository map

- `STATE.md`: authoritative project checkpoint and next actions.
- `RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md`: comparative grant-winner and research-discovery method.
- `GRANT_PROGRAMME.md`: programme facts, requirements, deadlines, and verification status.
- `research/`: landscape reviews, hypotheses, and topic selection.
- `sources/`: source ledger and bibliography.
- `data/`: datasets, metadata, and provenance notes.
- `analysis/`: methods, models, calculations, and reproducibility notes.
- `outputs/`: research papers, figures, briefs, and publication-ready materials.
